# Changelog

What changed for people using Sputnik, newest first. Code-only work stays in the git history. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and versions follow [SemVer](https://semver.org).

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Eight factory presets, from Machine Gun and Dying Battery to Bass Rocket and Wah Lead. Step through them with the arrows beside the footswitch, or click the name. Move any knob and it reads Custom.

## [0.1.1] - 2026-11-23

### Added
- Sputnik, a synth fuzz for guitar and bass, as a VST3 and CLAP plugin for macOS and Windows.
- Two modes: Sputter, a gated, choked square fuzz with a stutter gate synced to the host's tempo, and Synth, fuzz into a resonant filter swept by your playing, with a pitch-tracked oscillator blended in.
- A clean low-end crossover, so bass keeps its weight.
- A green phosphor oscilloscope that shows the real output.
